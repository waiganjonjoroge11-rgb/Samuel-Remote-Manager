Audio recording

Use Request audio recording on a device card. The phone app must be open. The phone owner must approve the on-phone prompt; this opens the recorder and starts only after microphone permission is granted if needed. A persistent microphone notification remains visible. Short recordings are saved in the owner’s private Firebase Realtime Database archive. A 6 MB file-size limit applies. Silent recording is not supported.

Camera sharing

Use “Request camera screen” on a device card to send an owner-confirmed request. The Samuel Remote Manager app must already be open on the phone to receive it. The owner must approve the prompt, grant camera permission if asked, and tap Start. A visible Android notification remains while sharing. This is a periodic still-image feed, not continuous video.

Security and privacy

This dashboard displays only data shared by a device signed into the same account. Location sharing must be started by the phone owner in the Android app and remains visible through an ongoing Android notification. Audio and camera capture require visible, on-device owner approval and remain visibly active. This dashboard does not secretly access a camera, microphone, screen, or device controls.
